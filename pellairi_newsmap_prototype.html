Decision infrastructure

Pre-Execution Decision Sandbox

A controlled workflow for testing a proposed action before execution by combining structured inputs, quantitative simulation, independent qualitative analysis, synthesis, adjudication, and an auditable record.

01 · STRUCTURE

Define

Represent the decision, variables, constraints, assumptions, models, and datasets in a structured form.

02 · ANALYZE

Test

Run quantitative simulation and independent supportive/adversarial analysis against the same defined decision.

03 · SYNTHESIZE

Review

Cross-check outputs, record uncertainty and sensitivity, and produce an auditable synthesis before execution.

Potential research workflow

Where structured tooling could reduce workload

The diagram below describes potential workload reduction, not a measured productivity claim. The intended principle is to automate repeatable organization and checking while keeping substantive judgment with the researcher.

01
CollectGather articles, datasets, documents, and metadata.
02
NormalizeStandardize fields, labels, timestamps, and source records.
03

04
EvaluateApply defined rubrics, tests, simulations, and consistency checks.
05
AuditPreserve provenance, decisions, outputs, and review history.
Potential workload effect: less repetitive searching, sorting, formatting, cross-referencing, and first-pass checking; more researcher time available for interpretation, experimental design, methodological review, and substantive conclusions. Actual savings would need to be measured against a defined baseline.
